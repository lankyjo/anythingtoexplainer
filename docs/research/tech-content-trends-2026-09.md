# Top 10 Tech Topics for Content Creators — September 2026

## Method & limits

"Hot" here means demonstrated demand, not importance: every ranking claim rests on first-party numbers from the source that owns them — platform trend reports (Google Year in Search, YouTube Culture & Trends, TikTok Next 2026), developer surveys (Stack Overflow 2025, JetBrains 2025), official release posts (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft), and registry/API download data (npm, Hugging Face) — plus adoption numbers as demand proxies where no audience metric exists. What could not be verified from a primary source is explicitly labeled `[secondary]` with the reason (paywall, corporate filing, executive post, or press reprint); no first-party platform publishes per-tech-topic video view counts, so YouTube/TikTok data is used for format-level evidence, not per-topic ranking. This is a mid-September 2026 snapshot: annual reports lag 1–11 months, download figures are last-30-day windows fetched 2026-09-18, and model/version names move on a monthly cadence — re-check anything before production.

## Ranking

### 1. Frontier AI models & the billion-user assistants (GPT-6 Astra, Gemini, Claude)
- **What it is**: The current generation of flagship models — OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (Sept 2026), Google's Gemini 3.x, and Anthropic's Fable/Mythos/Opus line — and the consumer assistants built on them. This is now the largest single audience in tech, not a niche.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - ChatGPT serves "more than 1 billion people"; Gemini app hit 950M monthly active users with DAU tripling year-over-year — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/) (Aug 6, 2026); [Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings remarks](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/) (Jul 22, 2026).
  - Gemini was the #1 trending global search of 2025 and DeepSeek #6 on the same list — [Google Year in Search 2025](https://trends.withgoogle.com/year-in-search/2025/) (Dec 2025), ranking verified via [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-reveals-the-top-searches-of-2025/563738/) (Dec 23, 2025) `[secondary]`.
  - GPT-6 Astra shipped Sept 3, 2026 and is OpenAI's first model at the "Critical" cybersecurity capability level — [OpenAI safety overview](https://openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra/) (Sep 3, 2026); Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.1/Mythos 5.1 the same month — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1) (Sep 2026).
  - ChatGPT crossed 900M weekly users and 50M+ consumer subscribers earlier in 2026 — [CNET reporting OpenAI's numbers](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/chatgpt-has-almost-1-billion-weekly-users/) (Feb 27, 2026) `[secondary]`.
- **Creator angle**: model-drop news reactions and "what it can actually do" demos (GPT-6 Astra documents, spreadsheets, computer use); the billion-user stat makes "how to use it" explainers mass-market, not dev-only.
- **Confidence**: high — user counts come directly from OpenAI and Alphabet, and search-trend ranking is Google's own data.

### 2. Agentic AI coding tools (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor)
- **What it is**: AI agents that write, run, and debug code with minimal human input, now the default way a large share of developers work. Content around these tools is the single deepest tutorial/news niche in tech.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - 84% of developers use or plan to use AI tools, 51% of professionals use them daily, and 31% already use AI agents at work (14.1% daily); 69% of agent users report productivity gains — [Stack Overflow 2025 Developer Survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/) (published Dec 29, 2025).
  - 1.1M+ public repos import an LLM SDK (+178% YoY), 36M new developers joined GitHub in a year, and 80% of new developers use Copilot in their first week — [GitHub Octoverse 2025](https://octoverse.github.com/) (Oct 28, 2025).
  - 85% of developers regularly use AI tools; 62% use at least one coding assistant, agent, or AI editor — [JetBrains State of Developer Ecosystem 2025](https://blog.jetbrains.com/research/2025/10/state-of-developer-ecosystem-2025/) (Oct 15, 2025).
  - Claude Code run-rate revenue passed $2.5B, doubled since Jan 1, 2026, with business subscriptions quadrupling — [Constellation Research citing Anthropic](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/anthropics-claude-code-revenue-doubled-jan-1) (Feb 12, 2026) `[secondary: company figure via analyst report]`.
- **Creator angle**: tool face-offs ("same app built by Claude Code vs Codex vs Cursor"), cost/benchmark experiments, and migration tutorials; the SO survey shows younger developers prefer interactive/social formats over static posts — [survey data](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/) (Dec 2025).
- **Confidence**: high — three independent first-party surveys (Stack Overflow, GitHub, JetBrains) plus a revenue datapoint.

### 3. AI video generation (Sora, Veo 3.1)
- **What it is**: Text/image-to-video models good enough for real short-form content, with Sora as the consumer app and Veo 3.1 embedded in YouTube's own creator tools. It directly changes how explainer and entertainment videos get made.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - OpenAI's Sora app passed 1M downloads in under five days — faster than ChatGPT's launch — and hit #1 on the US App Store within days — [CNBC quoting OpenAI's Sora lead](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/09/openais-sora-downloads.html) (Oct 9, 2025); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/03/openais-sora-soars-to-no-1-on-the-u-s-app-store/) (Oct 3, 2025) `[secondary: filings/executive posts are the primary record]`.
  - "Tens of millions" of videos had already been generated with Veo 3 by July 2025 — [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/veo-3-now-available-gemini-api/) (Jul 17, 2025).
  - Veo 3.1 added native 9:16 vertical output, 1080p/4K upscaling, and shipped into YouTube Shorts, YouTube Create, and the Gemini app — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/veo-3-1-ingredients-to-video/) (Jan 13, 2026).
- **Creator angle**: "I made this with Sora/Veo" builds, prompt-to-story workflow breakdowns, and reaction/ethics commentary (deepfakes, likeness rights) — the App Store data proves mainstream curiosity beyond tech audiences.
- **Confidence**: high — download and launch data are strong; the only soft spot is that app-store ranks are reported by third parties, not OpenAI.

### 4. Vibe coding & prompt-to-app building
- **What it is**: Building software by describing it in natural language instead of writing code — now a mainstream cultural term, not just a developer workflow. It splits audiences between "I built this without coding" and "here's why it breaks."
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - "Vibe coding" was named Collins Dictionary's Word of the Year 2025 after a sharp rise in usage since its coinage in Feb 2025 — [Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/woty) (Nov 6, 2025).
  - 84% of developers use or plan to use AI tools; meanwhile 72% say full prompt-to-app vibe coding is not part of their professional work, and 38% have no plans to adopt agents — [Stack Overflow blog](https://stackoverflow.blog/2025/12/29/developers-remain-willing-but-reluctant-to-use-ai-the-2025-developer-survey-results-are-here/) (Dec 29, 2025).
  - 80% of new GitHub developers use Copilot in their first week, and shell-script usage in AI-generated projects grew +206% YoY — code is increasingly written by agents for non-experts — [GitHub blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/typescript-python-and-the-ai-feedback-loop-changing-software-development/) (Nov 13, 2025).
- **Creator angle**: "I built an app in a weekend with zero code" challenges perform well, but so does the skeptic format ("what vibe coding gets wrong") — the 72% professional rejection is a ready-made counter-narrative.
- **Confidence**: high — dictionary-level cultural adoption plus first-party survey confirmation, with both pro and con angles documented.

### 5. MCP and the agent-tooling standard
- **What it is**: The Model Context Protocol, the de facto standard for connecting AI models to tools and data, now governed by a Linux Foundation body. It is the plumbing story behind every agent product, with a large tutorial economy around it.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - The official MCP SDK (`@modelcontextprotocol/sdk`) recorded 203,344,300 npm downloads in the last month — [npm registry API](https://api.npmjs.org/downloads/point/last-month/@modelcontextprotocol/sdk) (fetched Sep 18, 2026).
  - MCP was donated to the Linux Foundation's new Agentic AI Foundation, co-founded by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Block with AWS, Google, Microsoft, and Cloudflare backing — [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/841156/ai-companies-aaif-anthropic-mcp-model-context-protocol) (Dec 10, 2025); [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/agentic-ai-foundation/) (Dec 2025).
  - OpenAI adopted MCP in March 2025 and Google DeepMind followed in April 2025 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/09/google-says-itll-embrace-anthropics-standard-for-connecting-ai-models-to-data/) (Apr 9, 2025) `[secondary: announcements were executive posts]`.
- **Creator angle**: "What is MCP?" explainers, build-an-MCP-server tutorials, and security deep-dives (prompt injection through tools is the reliable scary episode).
- **Confidence**: high — download volume is first-party and enormous; governance and cross-vendor adoption are documented.

### 6. Open-weight & local AI (Qwen, DeepSeek, gpt-oss)
- **What it is**: Downloadable frontier-adjacent models that run on your own hardware or cheaply in your own cloud — the "own your AI" counter-current to API subscriptions. Tutorials about running them locally keep growing because the models keep improving.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - Qwen3.8-27B (Apache 2.0, Aug 2026) recorded 7,456,257 downloads in the last month — [Hugging Face API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B) (fetched Sep 18, 2026).
  - DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (MIT, released Sep 10, 2026) reached 390,657 downloads in its first week — [Hugging Face API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash) (fetched Sep 18, 2026).
  - OpenAI's open-weight gpt-oss-120b holds 5,238,070 downloads in the last month — [Hugging Face API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/openai/gpt-oss-120b) (fetched Sep 18, 2026).
  - DeepSeek was the #6 trending global Google search of 2025 — [Google Year in Search 2025](https://trends.withgoogle.com/year-in-search/2025/) (Dec 2025), via [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-reveals-the-top-searches-of-2025/563738/) (Dec 23, 2025) `[secondary]`.
- **Creator angle**: "run this on your laptop" benchmarks, VRAM/hardware buyer guides, and privacy/self-hosting angles; release cadence (monthly) gives an endless news hook.
- **Confidence**: medium — download counts are first-party and large, but they measure builders, not video viewers; audience demand is inferred from search interest rather than measured.

### 7. The Linux migration wave
- **What it is**: A sustained, measurable shift of desktop users toward Linux, driven by Windows 10's end of support and better gaming compatibility. It is a reliable story for both "I switched" vlogs and practical migration tutorials.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - Windows 10 support ended Oct 14, 2025, pushing users to upgrade, pay for ESU, or switch — [Microsoft end-of-support page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/end-of-support) (fetched Sep 18, 2026).
  - Linux hit a record 5.33% of Steam users in March 2026 and held 3.90% in August 2026 — [Steam Hardware & Software Survey](https://store.steampowered.com/hwsurvey/) (Aug 2026); record figure via [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Steam-Survey-August-2026) `[secondary: Steam does not publish historical commentary]`.
  - Linux reached 7.53% of worldwide desktop OS share in July 2026, within 0.1 points of macOS (7.61%) — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/desktop) (Jul 2026).
- **Creator angle**: "30 days on Linux" experiments, distro picks for Windows refugees (CachyOS and Bazzite are the fast-growing gaming picks per the Steam data), and old-PC revival builds.
- **Confidence**: high — two independent first-party trackers (Valve, StatCounter) plus Microsoft's own EOL page.

### 8. AI-powered cyberattacks and AI security
- **What it is**: AI is now visibly accelerating real attacks — vulnerability exploitation overtook stolen credentials as the top breach entry point, and employees leak data into unapproved AI tools. Security content has flipped from theory to incident-driven.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - 31% of breaches started with vulnerability exploitation, the first time in 19 years it surpassed stolen credentials; ransomware reached 48% of breaches and third-party involvement jumped 60% to 48% — [Verizon 2026 DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/reports/dbir/) (May 19, 2026).
  - "Shadow AI" tripled: 45% of employees are regular AI users on corporate devices (up from 15%), with source code the most common data submitted to unauthorized models — [Verizon 2026 DBIR PDF](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T1ae/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf) (May 2026).
  - OpenAI flagged GPT-6 Astra as its first "Critical" cybersecurity-capability model; Anthropic ships Mythos 5.1 only through a vetted cyberdefense program — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra/) (Sep 3, 2026); [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1) (Sep 2026).
- **Creator angle**: "AI vs hackers" explainers, live red-team/demo formats, and corporate-policy takes on shadow AI; the DBIR's numbers give every episode a hard stat.
- **Confidence**: high — Verizon's dataset is first-party, and the model-capability claims come straight from OpenAI and Anthropic.

### 9. Smart glasses & AI wearables
- **What it is**: Camera-and-AI glasses crossed from novelty to a real consumer category, led by Meta's Ray-Ban/Oakley line and now contested by Google/Samsung. They raise immediate privacy questions, which is exactly what makes them a content engine.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - EssilorLuxottica sold over 7M Meta AI glasses in 2025, more than tripling 2023+2024 combined — [CNBC reporting company results](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/ray-ban-maker-essilorluxottica-triples-sales-of-meta-ai-glasses.html) (Feb 11, 2026) `[secondary: company earnings release]`.
  - AI-glasses revenue "almost doubled" year-over-year in Q2 2026, which the company calls exponential growth — [EssilorLuxottica H1 2026 release, reprinted](https://invisionmag.com/essilorluxottica-h1-2026-revenue-up-9-7-myopia-sales-up-24-ai-glasses-nearly-double/) (Jul 31, 2026) `[secondary: press-release reprint; primary is the company's investor release]`.
  - Meta delayed the international launch of the $799 Ray-Ban Display due to "unprecedented" US demand, and is discussing capacity toward 20M units/year — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/ray-ban-maker-essilorluxottica-triples-sales-of-meta-ai-glasses.html) (Feb 11, 2026); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-13/meta-said-to-discuss-doubling-ray-ban-glasses-output-after-surge-in-demand) (Jan 13, 2026) `[secondary]`.
- **Creator angle**: "I wore smart glasses for a week" vlogs, hands-on demos around the next Meta Connect (Sept 23, 2026), and privacy/consent debates; supply shortages give the category built-in news hooks.
- **Confidence**: high — sales and revenue figures are company-reported and consistent across multiple outlets.

### 10. Humanoid robots
- **What it is**: Bipedal robots moved from demo reels to a public-market story in 2026, led by China's Unitree. It combines spectacle (kung-fu robots) with a real investability and jobs narrative.
- **Evidence it's hot**:
  - Unitree shipped 5,500+ humanoids in 2025 with 32.4% global share and quadrupled revenue to ¥1.7B, then filed for a Shanghai STAR IPO — [Reuters citing the prospectus](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/unitree-plans-shanghai-ipo-testing-interest-humanoid-robots-2026-03-20/) (Mar 20, 2026); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/chinese-humanoid-robot-maker-unitree-prices-ipo-at-9-billion-valuation.html) (Aug 6, 2026) `[secondary: primary is the listing prospectus]`.
  - The IPO priced at ~$9B and shares jumped more than 600% on debut, with subscriptions oversubscribed over 8,000x — a record for Shanghai's STAR Market — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/tech/china-unitree-ipo-intl-hnk) (Aug 18, 2026) `[secondary: exchange filing]`.
  - Unitree said cumulative bipedal production reached ~18,000 units by Aug 12, 2026; global humanoid shipments hit ~19,100 in H1 2026 with Chinese makers above 97% — [The Robotics Media](https://www.theroboticsmedia.com/article/unitree-18000-cumulative-bipedal-humanoid-production-g1-star-market-ipo-august-12-2026) (Aug 17, 2026) `[secondary: company claim and SAG tracker]`.
- **Creator angle**: robot-demo reactions and "how close are we really" explainers; the IPO turns it into a business/tech crossover story with daily price action as a hook.
- **Confidence**: medium — the event and IPO demand are well documented, but unit/deployment numbers come via secondary reporting of filings and company claims, and real factory deployment is still early.
